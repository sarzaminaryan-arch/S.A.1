# Article-image attributions

This document records eleven WebP photographs bundled with the child theme and sixteen Wikimedia photographs that the ready-article importer will copy into the local WordPress media library when the relevant drafts are imported. The importer records each source URL; published pages therefore do not hotlink Wikimedia. The eight bundled article-image WebPs were resized when needed, adjusted with Pillow (color ×1.07, contrast ×1.06, sharpness ×1.12), and saved as WebP, quality 82, method 6. The three Sar Agha Seyed derivatives were converted from Commons JPEGs to WebP (quality 86, method 6) without cropping, upscaling, or changing their pixel dimensions; the Mehr News watermark is retained. Every image remains under its source media license, and CC BY-SA derivatives retain the applicable ShareAlike license. These media files are not relicensed under the theme's software license.

## Wikimedia images copied into WordPress on article import

These sixteen images are referenced by their Wikimedia URLs in `data/ready-articles.php`. The importer copies each one to the local media library once and stores its source URL; they are not served from an external hotlink on the published page. The UNESCO images document the combined Tang-e-Sayad and Sabzkuh biosphere reserve; their captions explicitly avoid presenting them as a precise view inside the national park. The Persepolis griffin detail, Fin Garden, and Masouleh entries use 1280-pixel Wikimedia-generated thumbnails where the original files are large; the Azgen originals are 1000×667 pixels.
